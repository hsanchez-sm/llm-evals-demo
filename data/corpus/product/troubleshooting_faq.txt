EVALSINC — TROUBLESHOOTING FAQ
==============================

How do I reset my password?
  Go to the login page > "Forgot password". You'll receive a reset
  link by email, valid for 30 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check
  spam and confirm the email matches your account.

How do I generate an API key?
  Settings > API Keys > "Create key". Copy it immediately — it is
  shown only once. API access requires a Pro or Enterprise plan.

How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?
  Settings > Security > "Enable 2FA" and scan the QR code with an
  authenticator app. Save the backup codes.

My dashboard won't load / is blank.
  Hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R), clear cache, and disable ad blockers
  for the app domain. Check status.evalsinc.example for outages.

How do I connect an integration?
  Settings > Integrations > select the provider > "Connect", then
  authorize. If the connection drops, reconnect and re-authorize
  the requested scopes.

How do I add teammates?
  Settings > Members > "Invite". Seats are limited by your plan.
