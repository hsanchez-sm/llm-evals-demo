EVALSINC — PRICING & PLANS
==========================

EvalsInc offers three subscription tiers. Prices are in USD and
billed per user. Annual billing saves 20% versus monthly.

FREE
- Price: $0
- Users: 1
- Queries: 100 per month
- Support: community forum
- API access: no

PRO
- Price: $49 / user / month (or $470 / user / year)
- Users: up to 10
- Queries: 10,000 per month
- Support: email, next-business-day
- API access: yes

ENTERPRISE
- Price: custom (contact sales)
- Users: unlimited
- Queries: unlimited
- Support: dedicated success manager, 24/7, 99.9% uptime SLA
- API access: yes, with SSO and audit logs

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take
effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
