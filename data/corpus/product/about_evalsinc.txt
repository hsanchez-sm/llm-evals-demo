ABOUT THE EVALSINC ASSISTANT
============================

EvalsInc is a company-intelligence assistant. It answers questions
over a knowledge base of companies and their employees, and helps
with account and product questions.

What the assistant can do:
- Company & people lookup: facts about companies in the knowledge
  base (industry, size, revenue, website) and their employees
  (role, department, contact).
- Billing & plans: pricing, upgrades, refunds, invoices.
- Technical support: API errors, password resets, integrations,
  and general troubleshooting.
- General help: explain its own capabilities and point you to the
  right place.

What it cannot do:
- It does not give legal, financial, or investment advice.
- It only knows about companies that exist in its knowledge base.

Just ask in natural language — the assistant routes your question
to the right specialist automatically.
