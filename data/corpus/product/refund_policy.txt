EVALSINC — REFUND POLICY
========================

14-day money-back guarantee (Pro):
New Pro subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the
first charge. Request it from Billing > Help > "Request refund" or
by emailing billing@evalsinc.example.

Annual plans:
Cancelling an annual Pro plan after the 14-day window stops renewal
at the end of the paid term. Unused months are not refunded unless
required by local law.

Enterprise:
Enterprise contracts are governed by the signed order form. Custom
terms there override this policy.

Duplicate or erroneous charges:
If you were charged twice for the same period, contact billing and
the duplicate charge is reversed within 5–7 business days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
