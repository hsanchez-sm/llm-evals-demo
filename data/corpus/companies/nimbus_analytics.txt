COMPANY PROFILE — Nimbus Analytics
===================================

Company name:   Nimbus Analytics, Inc.
Website:        https://www.nimbusanalytics.io
Industry:       Data Analytics SaaS
Headquarters:   Austin, Texas, USA
Founded:        2016
Employees:      240
Annual revenue: USD 48 million (FY2024)

About:
Nimbus Analytics is a cloud platform that turns raw business
data into real-time dashboards. Its flagship product, NimbusBoard,
lets non-technical teams build live metrics views without writing
SQL. The company serves mid-market retail and fintech customers
across North America.

Leadership:
- Maya Okonkwo — CEO & Co-founder
- Raj Patel — Head of Data Science
