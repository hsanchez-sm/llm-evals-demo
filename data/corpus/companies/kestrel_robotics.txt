COMPANY PROFILE — Kestrel Robotics
==================================

Company name:   Kestrel Robotics GmbH
Website:        https://www.kestrelrobotics.ai
Industry:       Industrial Robotics
Headquarters:   Munich, Germany
Founded:        2019
Employees:      85
Annual revenue: EUR 12 million (FY2024)

About:
Kestrel Robotics designs autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for
warehouse logistics. Its robots navigate cluttered floors to move
inventory between picking stations, integrating with existing
warehouse management systems via an open API.

Leadership:
- Aiko Tanaka — Chief Technology Officer & Co-founder
- Daniel Brooks — Lead Robotics Engineer
