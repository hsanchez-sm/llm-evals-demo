COMPANY PROFILE — Verdant Foods
===============================

Company name:   Verdant Foods, LLC
Website:        https://www.verdantfoods.com
Industry:       Organic Food Manufacturing
Headquarters:   Portland, Oregon, USA
Founded:        2009
Employees:      1,150
Annual revenue: USD 310 million (FY2024)

About:
Verdant Foods produces organic packaged foods — cereals, snack
bars, and plant-based meal kits — distributed to grocery chains
across North America. The company operates three production
facilities and is certified USDA Organic and B-Corp.

Leadership:
- Lena Hoffmann — Chief Operating Officer
- Tomás Rivera — Supply Chain Manager
